    items:
      - question: Payroll compliance and governance for HR and Finance
        answer: >-
          For leaders and managers with payroll accountability. Explore how workforce decisions, payroll
          hand-offs and approval processes affect pay outcomes. Work through responsibilities, risk visibility
          and escalation using a practical scenario. A proposed exercise is a responsibilities map and a
          30-day action plan for your team.
      - question: Payroll risk and controls
        answer: >-
          For payroll managers, HR operations and Finance teams. Identify risks across the payroll cycle,
          distinguish a control from an informal check, and discuss what evidence shows a control is working.
          A proposed exercise is a prioritised risk register with control owners, review points and escalation
          routes. More detailed work can be scoped across multiple sessions.
      - question: Understanding and preventing payroll underpayments
        answer: >-
          For teams responsible for payroll data, policies, processes and systems. Use a fictional underpayment
          scenario to investigate the underlying driver, identify who needs to act and decide how to check that
          the issue has been resolved. A proposed exercise is a root-cause worksheet and a prevention action plan.
      - question: Remediation results and validation
        answer: >-
          For remediation leads, payroll specialists and Finance reviewers. Discuss data completeness,
          unexplained variances, validation responsibilities and the evidence needed before approval.
          A tailored session can explore previously remediated periods and prior-payment reconciliation.
          Example exercises include a validation approach and an exception log, with scope agreed around
          participants' experience.
      - question: Payroll governance during system change
        answer: >-
          For project sponsors, payroll owners and implementation teams. Explore decision ownership,
          testing responsibilities, issue escalation and readiness evidence. A proposed exercise is a set of
          governance checkpoints for your project, linked to the people accountable for each decision.
      - question: Payroll leadership and executive communication
        answer: >-
          For senior payroll leaders and executive sponsors. Practise explaining payroll risk, separating
          unresolved issues from accepted risks, and making the decision required clear. A proposed exercise
          is a concise risk briefing or board-paper outline.
