  - _type: faqSection
    heading: Questions before we begin
    items:
      - question: What is payroll remediation?
        answer: >-
          Payroll remediation is the work of identifying and correcting employee underpayments and
          addressing their underlying causes. It connects scope, data, calculations, validation,
          communication, payment and measures to prevent recurrence.
      - question: Can you help with an existing remediation program?
        answer: >-
          Yes. We can discuss targeted support at the point your program needs it, such as investigating
          findings, strengthening validation, reconciling prior payments or coordinating employee outcomes.
      - question: Do you provide the calculation platform or run our payroll?
        answer: >-
          TPS provides advisory, program management, validation and coordination support. We can work with
          calculation providers such as Yellow Canary. We do not supply their calculation engine or take
          over routine payroll processing. Agree responsibility for methodology, calculations, approvals
          and payment execution in the engagement scope.
      - question: Can you support re-remediation of historical periods?
        answer: >-
          Yes. We can help plan the review, validate revised results and develop a traceable approach to
          prior-payment reconciliation. Whether a historical period needs review, and the permitted
          treatment of earlier payments, must be established for your circumstances with relevant advisers.
      - question: How is this different from a payroll compliance review?
        answer: >-
          A review investigates risk or discrepancies. Remediation support helps the organisation act on
          those findings through corrective actions, validation, communications and payment coordination.
          If the issue is not yet understood, a diagnostic review may be the starting point.
      - question: Can program management and independent oversight be combined?
        answer: >-
          The roles must be defined clearly. Managing a workstream is different from independently reviewing
          that work. We agree delivery responsibilities, review boundaries and any required separation at
          the outset so the organisation knows what assurance it is receiving.
      - question: What should we bring to the first conversation?
        answer: >-
          A summary of the issue, your program stage, the teams and providers involved, and the decisions
          causing difficulty. You do not need to send employee payroll records through the contact form.
          Scope, access and information-sharing arrangements are agreed before detailed work begins.
      - question: Does this replace legal or tax advice?
        answer: >-
          No. Legal interpretation, tax treatment and other specialist decisions need the appropriate
          advisers. We coordinate their decisions within the program; the engagement does not guarantee
          compliance or regulator acceptance.
