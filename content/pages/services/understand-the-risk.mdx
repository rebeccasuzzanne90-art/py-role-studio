  - _type: textBlock
    heading: What happens in a payroll risk review
    body: |
      **1. Agree the questions and scope.** Discuss your concerns, entities, systems, teams and available evidence. Set boundaries, access requirements, deliverables and timing before work begins.

      **2. Follow the process and evidence.** Review the agreed records, speak with the people responsible and trace the handoffs that influence pay. Record limitations and matters needing specialist assessment.

      **3. Prioritise findings with your team.** Distinguish confirmed issues from potential risks, agree owners and identify urgent decisions alongside longer-term control improvements.

      **4. Support the next steps.** Help translate findings into action, with follow-up support agreed to suit your needs.

      For example, a role change may pass through HR, a manager and Payroll without anyone confirming whether the employee’s classification needs review. We help identify where the decision or handoff is unclear, who needs to act and what evidence would show the revised process is working.

  - _type: faqSection
    heading: Questions about a payroll compliance review
    items:
      - question: Is this the same as a payroll audit?
        answer: The scope matters more than the label. Our review examines agreed payroll risks, processes, ownership and controls. A detailed recalculation of employee entitlements, a formal assurance opinion or legal advice is not automatically included. We agree any required calculation testing and specialist involvement before work starts.
      - question: Can you help with both underpayment and overpayment issues?
        answer: Yes. We can investigate the process, data, policy or system drivers behind either issue and help strengthen controls. The treatment of employee amounts, including any proposed recovery, needs an agreed approach and appropriate specialist advice.
      - question: What happens if you identify an underpayment?
        answer: We help clarify the next investigation and decisions. TPS can also lead an end-to-end remediation program, coordinating specialists, calculations, validation, communications and payment delivery under an agreed scope.
      - question: How long does a review take?
        answer: Timing depends on the entities, systems, processes and records in scope, the availability of your team and the testing required. We agree milestones and dependencies after the initial scoping conversation.
      - question: Can you work with our existing advisers and payroll provider?
        answer: Yes. We clarify responsibilities and coordinate their input. Where independent assurance is required, its scope and separation from delivery work must be explicit.
      - question: What should we bring to the first conversation?
        answer: A summary of the issues, systems and teams involved, previous findings and any important deadlines. You do not need to send employee payroll records through the enquiry form.
